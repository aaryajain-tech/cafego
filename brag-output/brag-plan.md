# CafeGo — Launch Video Plan (/brag)

## 1. Project Overview & Planning Rubric
1. **What does the app actually do?**
   CafeGo is a friend-powered cafe and restaurant discovery application rendered as an interactive, full-page 3D WebGL spatial world with Augmented Reality (AR) tabletop dish inspection. It replaces fake 5-star Google reviews with authentic verdicts from your real friends.
2. **Core Problem Solved:**
   Fake online reviews and sponsored food influencers trick people into visiting mediocre spots.
3. **Target Audience:**
   Urban foodies, specialty coffee lovers, brunch squads, and remote workers.
4. **Standout Highlights:**
   - Fullscreen 3D sage-minimalist cafe with Barista Trishi and the famous wavy wall (*"Maybe coffee is addicted to me?"*).
   - Conversational cloud speech bubbles with candid friend reviews.
   - Live AR tabletop dish inspection (Kimchi Croissant, Cardamom Espresso Tonic).
   - Friend Squads with shared taste circles and coffee tokens.
5. **Visual Personality:**
   Tactile sage green (`#9BB196`), warm oak, cream plaster, claymorphism, and playful cloud bubbles.
6. **Hook (First 2.5s):**
   *“Google gave it 4.9 stars. The matcha was pure powdered syrup.”*
7. **Core Claim:**
   *“Stop trusting strangers. Taste the truth with your friends.”*
8. **Outro / CTA:**
   *“Form your squad. Discover CafeGo.”*
9. **Tone & Audio:**
   - Tone: `default` (playful, crisp, postable, high energy, honest foodie spirit).
   - Duration: 18.0 seconds.
   - Music: Upbeat indie lo-fi beat with crisp claps and smooth bassline.

---

## 2. Beat-by-Beat Storyboard (18.0s Total)

### Beat 1: The Pain Hook (0.0s – 2.5s)
- **Visual:** Clean split comparison. On the left: a generic Google review flashing fake 5-star emojis with a red warning badge: *"Account created yesterday · Paid promo"*.
- **Motion:** The fake review card shakes and cracks away.
- **Copy:** *"Tired of fake 5-star stranger reviews on Google?"*
- **SFX:** Muffled static glitch into a crisp vinyl record drop.

### Beat 2: The 3D Spatial Reveal (2.5s – 7.0s)
- **Visual:** Camera swoops down into the full 3D CafeGo world. Warm sage fluted counter, illuminated CafeGo sign, and Barista Trishi waving behind the bar.
- **Motion:** Isometric orbit camera rotates around the wavy wall with snake plants and *"Maybe coffee is addicted to me?"*.
- **Copy:** *"Meet CafeGo — The 3D friend-powered cafe discovery engine."*
- **SFX:** Soft cafe espresso hiss + chime.

### Beat 3: Candid Cloud Bubble Reviews (7.0s – 11.5s)
- **Visual:** The 3D avatar walks up to Elena at the laptop bench and Sarah at the booth. Puffy cloud speech bubbles pop up overhead:
  - *Elena: “180 Mbps Wi-Fi & whisper quiet.”*
  - *Sarah: “Skip the syrup matcha! Kimchi Croissant is 10/10 elite.”*
- **Motion:** Floating cloud bobbing, smooth scale-in pops.
- **Copy:** *"No bots. Just friends whose tastebuds you actually trust."*
- **SFX:** Tactile pop clicks and footstep beats.

### Beat 4: AR Tabletop Dish Inspection (11.5s – 15.0s)
- **Visual:** Phone camera pass-through activates: a 3D golden flaky Kimchi Croissant and steaming Cardamom Espresso Tonic spin in real Augmented Reality on an actual table.
- **Motion:** 360° AR turntable spin + glowing match badge: *98% Friend Match*.
- **Copy:** *"Inspect secret fusion menus in real AR before you visit."*
- **SFX:** Camera shutter click + glass clink.

### Beat 5: Outro & Punchline (15.0s – 18.0s)
- **Visual:** High-impact claymorphic lockup with the CafeGo brand mark, steam badge, and invite link: `cafego.app`.
- **Copy:** *"Form your squad. Taste the truth. CafeGo."*
- **CTA:** *"Join the circle today."*
- **SFX:** Upbeat resolved chord + warm coffee pour.
